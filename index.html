How I work with AI

Human in the loop

AI proposes, a person approves. Anything applied automatically is marked and undoable in one tap.

The model doesn't decide

It returns candidates; deterministic code decides what passes — with evals as the gate.

Only true claims

Guardrails block any number, price or urgency that isn't in the facts.

Privacy by default

On-device processing where possible, logs without content, extra protection for children.

Measure what matters

Paying users, not likes; a holdout group, not just conversions.

Small rounds, a human "ship it"

Every change on a branch with a preview; production only after approval.
